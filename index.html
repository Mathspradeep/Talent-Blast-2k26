General Rules

  • The performance duration should be 3 to 4 minutes.
  • Participants must submit their audio track (MP3 format) to the coordinators at least 30 minutes before the event.
  • Any dance style is permitted (Freestyle, Classical, Folk, Contemporary, Hip-Hop, etc.).
  • Songs containing obscene, vulgar, or abusive lyrics are strictly prohibited.
  • Participants are responsible for their own costumes and props. Props must be safe, easy to set up, and remove.
  • Dangerous items such as fire, glass, sharp objects, smoke effects, or fireworks are strictly banned.
  • Participants must report to the venue 15 minutes before their scheduled slot.
  • The judges' decision will be final and binding.

Judging Criteria (Total: 100 Marks)
